V12 engine recording

V12 Ferrari F93A @ Zandvoort by Pe, Italia a Zandvoort 2013.

Edits: short sections at 2.65–2.95 and 3.75–4.01 seconds were mixed to mono, filtered, resampled to stabilize their rising pitch, crossfaded into loops and normalized. Playback pitch and volume follow the car's RPM and throttle.

This is a hybrid voice: the recording supplies acceleration at upper revs; synthesis supplies low revs and coasting, and remains available if the recording cannot load. The reference RPMs are estimates from recorded pitch, without a tachometer. Other engine eras currently use synthesis.
